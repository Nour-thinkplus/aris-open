# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in
our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
level of experience, gender identity and expression, age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, national identity, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open,
welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Empathy and kindness toward other people
- Respectfulness of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully receiving constructive feedback
- Focusing on contributing to the community, not just personal gain
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Use of overt sexual language or imagery
- Degrading, mocking, or derisive comments
- Personal attacks (trolling, insulting/derisive comments)
- Publishing others' private information (location, work email, etc.)
- Blocking or consistently excluding someone from a conversation
- Unwelcome touching or physical contact

When a contributor is violating the rules, the project maintainers may
take action to make the community feel safe:

1. **Correction** — a private, written note from a maintainer.
2. **Warning** — a written warning with a defined expected behaviour
   and a follow-up.
3. **Inactivity** — a temporary or permanent pause of the contributor's
   activity in public spaces of the project.
4. **Removal** — in extreme cases, the contributor is asked to remove
   their prior contributions.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All GitHub, Gitea, GitLab spaces of the project (issues, PRs, code,
  comments, wiki, etc.).
- Community channels (Discord, Matrix, Slack, etc.) when in an official
   capacity.
- Any public event (conference, meetup) where a community member is
  representing the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may
be reported by opening an issue labelled `conduct` or by emailing a
project maintainer directly. All complaints will be received and handled
responsibly. All maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and
security of anyone reporting an incident.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our
standards of acceptable behaviour. They will take appropriate and fair
corrective action in response to any behaviour that they deem
inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.
